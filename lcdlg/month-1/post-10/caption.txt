Christmas at the finca.

Twelve of you across the two houses. The lamb in the wood oven, the long table laid for everyone. A tree from the Santa Maria market. Midnight on Christmas Eve, and the brave ones in the sea on Boxing Day.

The chef cooks the two big days or the kitchen is yours. Palma's lights are 25 minutes away. Christmas and New Year have a five-night minimum, and the last dates are going now.
