Boat days, the November edition.

The sea still holds the summer's warmth, the harbours are quiet, and the coves under Formentor that take a queue in August take nobody at all. A llaüt, the Mallorcan wooden boat, from Port de Pollença; a skipper who picks the calm days and knows which cove is empty on which day; lunch at anchor.

Thirty-five minutes from the house. We arrange the boat, the skipper and the lunch.
