November gives the island back.

The coaches have gone, the sea is still around 20 degrees, and the light turns gold at five instead of nine. This is the month the people who live here wait for: long lunches that drift, a late swim, a sky that goes on for ever.

Five bedrooms, a pool, seven and a half hectares, 25 minutes from Palma. November and December dates are open.
